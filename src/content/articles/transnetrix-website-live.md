Transnetrix now has a home online at [transnetrix.com.pk](https://transnetrix.com.pk). It sets out what we do and how we work for public institutions, development partners and businesses across Pakistan, from a base in Gilgit Baltistan.

## What you will find

**Four practices and one advisory core.** [Marcom](/practices/marcom/) for communication that changes behaviour, [EdTech](/practices/edtech/) for digital learning designed around adoption, [FinTech](/practices/fintech/) for financial services people actually use, and [IT Solutions](/practices/it-solutions/) for systems that work where the road ends. [Advisory](/practices/advisory/) holds them together: policy, research, programme management and capacity building.

**The record behind the work.** Two flagship engagements, the [Roads and Bridges Master Plan and O&M Policy](/work/roads-bridges-master-plan/) for Gilgit Baltistan and the [Social Health Protection Initiative Phase IV feasibility](/work/shpi-phase-iv-feasibility/), alongside the [wider experience record](/work/#experience).

**Legacy.** A page on [Abdul Hameed Khawar](/legacy/), whose history of the peoples of Gilgit Baltistan was compiled by his son Abdul Waheed and published posthumously.

## What comes next

This page is where articles and updates will appear. Articles will look at communication for development, evidence and delivery in mountain regions. News will cover new engagements and partnerships.
